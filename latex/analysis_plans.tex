\section*{About this document}
The analyses of the paper were fixed in three plans before the data they concern were generated or, for the first plan, before
the pooled results were seen. This document gives all three:

\smallskip
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l X p{3.3cm} l@{}}
\toprule
Part & Plan & Fixed & Evidence \\
\midrule
A & Families F1--F3 (blur, realistic noise, paired TCIA data), secondary analyses, descriptive NLST analysis &
1 Nov 2026, 11:30 (rev.~1); 20:01 (rev.~2) & git commits \texttt{5d5bb18}, \texttt{0b2973e} \\
B & Family F4: noise texture at equal level & 2 Nov 2026, 16:58 & SHA-256 record \\
C & Families F5--F7: in-plane blur, noise level, real reconstruction kernels; second training seed & 2 Nov 2026, 20:31 &
SHA-256 record \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\smallskip
Times are local (UTC+3). Parts B and C reproduce the plan files word for word; only the layout was changed (Markdown
converted to typeset text). The original files are in \texttt{plans\_original/} of this repository, and their SHA-256 hashes
can be checked against the values recorded before any of their data were generated (the record files are in
\texttt{hash\_records/}):

The record times are file times of the hash files written next to the analysis outputs on the first author's computer; like the git
commit times of Part A, they are not external, notarised time stamps. Each plan is followed by notes written afterwards
(``Notes''), which list deviations and analyses added after the data were seen; the paper labels these as not pre-specified.
